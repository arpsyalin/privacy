Information we handle

生日提醒 / Birthday Reminder, provided by arpslyl, stores the names, birthdays, calendar choices, notes and reminder settings you enter or import. Language, theme and layout preferences are also stored locally. There is no account, advertising or analytics tracking. We do not upload your birthday records to our servers or sell them.

Storage and protection

Records are stored in the app’s private storage. The Android app excludes its data from system cloud backup and device migration. Keep a manual backup before replacing your phone or uninstalling. Local storage and .birthday files are not separately password-encrypted by this app; protect your device and backup files.

Import, export and sharing

The app reads only files you choose to import or open with it. Export and sharing happen only at your request. A .birthday file contains the selected records, including names, birthdays and notes, in readable form. Your chosen file provider or receiving app may store or transmit it under its own policy. Share only records you are entitled to share, with trusted recipients. We cannot revoke copies already shared.

Permissions and reminders

Alarm-style reminders are optional. Saving a birthday with reminders enabled checks the following permissions and settings; missing items open the guide, also available in My profile. “Enable missing settings” opens missing settings one by one, not an automatic grant or blanket consent. You decide in each system screen and can decline or later revoke access. Birthdays are saved even if setup is incomplete, with a warning that the alarm may not ring. You can still manage, import and export records. The app does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone or broad file access.

Reminder permissions you control

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): display local birthday reminders. Denial prevents normal reminder notifications. Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): schedule reminders for the time you choose. Without access, precisely timed alarm reminders cannot be scheduled. Full-screen reminders (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): show the alarm page, potentially waking the locked screen, when a reminder is due. Without access, the page may not appear automatically. Names and reminder text may be visible to people nearby, including on the full-screen alarm page; hiding notification previews alone does not hide that page. Disable reminders if you do not want this display. Battery optimization exemption (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS): ask the system to allow background reminder operation. Refusing it may delay or prevent alarms; granting it can increase battery use. System versions and manufacturers may offer different controls.

Notification, sound and manufacturer settings

The guide also checks settings that are not separate runtime permissions. Notification channel: enable birthday alarm notifications and their importance/lock-screen display; disabling them can hide notifications or full-screen alerts. Alarm volume: the app reads the alarm stream volume and mute state to flag inaudible alarms. You adjust volume in system settings; the app does not raise it automatically or record sound. Manufacturer settings: check auto-start, unrestricted background operation, lock-screen/banner notifications, locking the app in recent tasks, and allowing alarms during Do Not Disturb where available. These switches cannot be reliably detected; “I have checked” records only your manual confirmation, not verified authorization. The confirmation remains locally until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. You can revisit these settings through the guide. Permission and setting checks are processed on the device, not uploaded or used for advertising or tracking. Even after all checks, power-saving policies, force-stop and other system restrictions can prevent or delay alarms.

How background alarms work

Android also grants supporting manifest permissions without separate runtime prompts: FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK keep alarm preparation/playback running with a foreground-service notification; WAKE_LOCK keeps the processor awake while preparing or playing an alarm; VIBRATE provides vibration; RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED restores scheduled reminders after restart. App updates may also restore reminders. These capabilities are used for reminders you enable, not continuous background data collection. Names/reminder text, trigger times and repeat/acknowledgment state are stored privately on the device for alarm delivery and recovery. While handling an alarm, screen-off/unlock events, audio focus and interactions with the alarm page control sound and retries; they are not used to track activity in other apps. “Stop sound” stops playback; “Got it” ends the active reminder occurrence. Simply switching the screen off can trigger another alert after five minutes, up to three retries. Disable a birthday’s reminders or delete the record to cancel its scheduled reminders; system settings let you revoke user-controlled permissions.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them. Delete individual records or use “Clear birthday data” in My profile to remove all birthday records and cancel their reminders; appearance and language preferences remain. Temporary export/share copies are cleared on the next app start or when clearing birthday data. System “Clear storage” or uninstalling removes the app’s local data. Copies exported to other folders or apps must be deleted separately. No account deletion is needed because no account is created.

Third-party components

The app uses local database, file picker, system share sheet, notification and timezone components to provide its features. It has no advertising or analytics SDK. Opening a policy website or contacting us by email uses services outside the app; their operators may process connection information under their own policies. Do not send birthday files or other personal data when requesting support unless necessary.
